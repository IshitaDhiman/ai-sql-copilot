# Sample Business Queries

## Customer

- Show all customers from Bangalore.
- List Premium customers.
- Customers with credit score above 750.

---

## Account

- Show all Savings accounts.
- Accounts with balance above ₹5,00,000.
- Average account balance by branch.

---

## Loan

- Active Home loans.
- Loans above ₹20,00,000.
- Average EMI amount.

---

## Transactions

- Highest transaction.
- UPI transactions above ₹10,000.
- Monthly spending by customer.

---

## Credit Card

- Expired cards.
- Highest credit limit.
- Online shopping transactions.

---

## Advanced Queries

- Customers having both Home Loan and Credit Card.
- Top 5 branches by loan amount.
- Customers with no transactions in the last 90 days.
- Employees earning above department average.
- Customers with more than one active account.
